# Project Strategy

## 🎯 Strategic Intent
Our goal is to dominate the local Kubernetes IDE market by focusing on the intersection of **System Hardware Management** and **DevOps Workflows**.

## 🗺️ Execution Pillars
1. **The Bridge Pattern:** Focus heavily on the Tauri-Rust bridge to ensure real-time OS-level hardware telemetry (GPU/RAM) can drive Kubernetes state.
2. **Monkey-Proof UI:** Invest in Vue 3.5's reactivity and AI-driven insights to hide K8s complexity until explicitly requested.
3. **Extensibility First:** Build a VSCode-like marketplace from day one to allow the community to add custom resource management plugins.

## 📈 Success Metrics
- **Resource Efficiency:** Latency of pod suspension when a heavy local process is detected.
- **System Stability:** Resource overhead of the StreamK8s application itself (Target: < 100MB RAM idle).
- **Ease of Use:** Time-to-first-pod-view for new users (Target: < 30 seconds from install).
