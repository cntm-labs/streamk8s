# Project Manifesto

## 📢 Core Beliefs
1. **Hardware is Sovereign:** The user's local hardware (GPU/RAM) should always prioritize their immediate activity (gaming, creation) without manual intervention.
2. **Kubernetes for All:** K8s management should be "Monkey-Proof"—intuitive enough for anyone to orchestrate workloads.
3. **The Intelligent Bridge:** We believe in a future where AI handles the negotiation between background workloads and foreground performance.

## 🎯 Our Aim
We build StreamK8s to liberate developers from the constant struggle of resource management. No more manual pod stopping before launching a game; no more GPU memory errors because of a forgotten background trainer. 

We leverage **Rust, Tauri, and Vue** to provide the speed, safety, and beauty required for a modern OS-level tool.
